The Folio Gazette · Issue 12

Summer Market

Drag me

Stalls open at nine along the river walk, with bread still warm from the oven, the first strawberries of the season and a brass band that plays until the lanterns come on. Bring a basket and a friend.

New this year: a printing corner where you can design a poster in minutes and take it home on heavy cream card. Kids get a free sheet of stickers, and every stall takes cards.

Parking is free after six. Follow the bunting from the station, or take the little ferry that leaves every twenty minutes from the old bridge.

At noon the bakers’ guild judges the great loaf contest, and at four the river choir sings from the bandstand. The cheese tent stays cool all day, and the lemonade stand gives refills to anyone wearing a straw hat.

Stallholders can still book a pitch for the autumn market. Pick up a form at the information kiosk by the clock tower, or print one at the printing corner while you wait for your poster.

Install in a minute

  1. Run the downloaded Folio-Publisher-Setup file.
  2. Allow it. If SmartScreen says “Windows protected your PC”, choose More info → Run anyway — the installer isn’t code-signed.
  3. Done. Folio is in the Start menu and opens .folio files.
  1. Drag Folio Publisher from the disk image into Applications.

  3. Done. You only need to do this once.

Questions

Is Folio free?

Yes. Download it and use it for personal and business work.

Can it open Microsoft Publisher (.pub) files?

No. Folio is its own app with its own .folio format. Share finished work as PDF or images.

Does it work offline?

Completely. Templates, fonts and all 85 spelling dictionaries are installed with the app.

Why does Windows or macOS warn me when I install it?

The installers aren’t code-signed yet. Follow the install steps above — you only need to allow it once.
